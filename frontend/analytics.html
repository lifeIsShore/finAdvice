How the AI Decides

Key Features used

  • Price Momentum: Recent returns (Lag 1-3) help AI detect trend strength.
  • Technical Indicators: RSI (Overbought/Oversold) and MACD (Trend reversals).
  • Standard Volatility: Helps AI calculate safety margins for Stop Losses.
  • Volume Analysis: Confirms if a move is backed by market participation.

News Sentiment Logic

The AI scrapes the latest 10 news articles for the asset and calculates an average sentiment score (-1 to +1). This acts as a multiplier:

Positive news (>0.3 score) ➔ AI may slightly increase Bullish targets.
Negative news (<-0.3 score) ➔ AI may lower targets or suggest tighter Stop Losses.

Winner Selection

The "Winner" is not just the most accurate. It's chosen using a weighted score:

  • 80% Weight: Trend Direction Accuracy.
  • 20% Weight: RMSE (how close the price prediction was).
